## Method note: when things are verified

Under VLBV a change is *implemented* in its round and *verified* once, at the boundary (B4), after every change is implemented; failures there go to an authorized repair (B5, max 2). This applies to change 1 too: its fix to the test command and the two timing tests is generated code and is first executed at B4. Running the **unchanged** pre-change suite is not generated-code execution; that baseline was recorded at assess (3,700 pass / 2 fail / 27 skip) and its 3,729 test IDs are fixed as a fixture (`fixtures/baseline-test-ids.txt`, sha256 cc4fdcce…34a6) so the boundary can prove no test was removed. Risk accepted by this ordering: a red baseline is discovered at B4 rather than earlier; mitigation is that change 1 is small and B5 repair is budgeted.
